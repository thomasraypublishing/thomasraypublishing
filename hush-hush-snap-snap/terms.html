3. Description of Service

Hush Hush Snap Snap is a camera app for iPhone with an Apple Watch companion. The App provides single-shot, burst, intervalometer, and video timers with independent visual, audio, and haptic countdown cues, supports every focal length your iPhone exposes, and includes on-device features such as scene-aware capture assistance, optional noise reduction, optional adaptive defaults that learn your preferences over time, and Apple Watch remote shutter control. Pro features are available via an optional one-time in-app purchase.

8. In-App Purchases

Hush Hush Snap Snap offers an optional one-time Pro upgrade with a 7-day free trial. All in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and are subject to Apple's terms and conditions.

The App is free to download and includes a Pro upgrade unlocked via a single non-consumable in-app purchase ("Pro"). Pro is offered with a 7-day free trial and is a one-time purchase priced at the amount displayed in the App Store at the time of purchase. Pro unlocks per-lens default durations, custom timer durations longer than 60 seconds, the intervalometer, the video timer, custom haptic patterns, capture history, Adaptive Defaults (on-device learning of your capture preferences), and Apple Watch features such as Crown scrubbing past 60 seconds. The free tier remains fully functional on its own. Pro is restorable on any device signed in with the same Apple ID via the "Restore Purchases" button. Family Sharing is enabled.

For refund requests, contact Apple Support. Thomas Ray Publishing does not directly process payments or refunds.
