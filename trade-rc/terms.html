3. Description of Service

Trade RC is an 18+ dating and chat-room application that brings IRC's text-first culture forward into a modern queer-focused dating product. 8. Reporting, Blocking, and Moderation

The App includes user-controlled safety tools and developer-operated moderation:

  • Block — You can block any user at any time from their profile or via room moderation tools. A user you block can no longer message you, see your profile, or be matched with you.
  • Report — You can report any user, room, or message to us using the in-app report flow. Reports include a category and an optional context note. We review every report on a reasonable timeline based on severity. Critical safety issues (threats of violence, CSAM, doxxing) are escalated immediately.
  • Channel moderation — Public room owners and operators may set channel modes (+m moderated, +i invite-only, +t topic-locked, +s secret, +v voiced), enforce topics, kick or ban abusive members, and mute users in the rooms they own.
  • On-device safety checks — Outgoing direct-message text and photos are screened locally on your device by on-device safety classifiers (a binary abuse-text classifier and Apple's Sensitive Content Analysis for photos, where available) before sending. None of this analysis runs on our servers; the result of the check stays on your device.
  • Developer moderation — Thomas Ray Publishing may remove User Content from public surfaces, suspend accounts, ban accounts, restrict features, withhold purchases, and refuse service at our sole discretion, including without prior notice for serious violations. Because direct messages are end-to-end encrypted, we cannot read their content; moderation actions affecting DMs are based on user reports of specific messages, on the metadata we still receive (sender, recipient, timestamp), and on the on-device safety classifiers described above.

Once an account is banned for a serious violation, you may not create a new account on Trade RC. Creating a new account to evade a ban is itself a violation.

The phrase-based companions (Host, Dot, Sage, Juke, Prompt, Keep, and the free Mercy companion) respond from pre-authored phrase pools with no AI model invoked. Omega-1 is the exception: it uses a third-party AI service and is described in the next bullet.
  • Mercy is free — the Mercy companion is built into the App at no charge and is available in every channel.
  • Omega-1 (third-party AI) — Omega-1 is an optional AI companion available exclusively to active Premium subscribers. You summon it in a public chat room with the /omega command. Unlike the other companions, Omega-1's replies are generated by Anthropic's Claude AI model: when you use Omega-1, the message you type plus recent messages from that public room are sent to Anthropic to generate the reply, which is then posted publicly in the room. Before your first use, Trade RC shows a consent screen that names the AI provider and explains that your messages leave your device; Omega-1 stays unavailable until you accept. Omega-1 is a fictional AI character — it is not a person or a licensed professional, cannot provide medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice, and its responses may be inaccurate. Do not rely on Omega-1 for professional, medical, or safety decisions. See our Privacy Policy (Section 9a) for the data shared when you use Omega-1.
  • Account deletion — if you delete your Trade RC account, your companion entitlements remain tied to your Apple ID and can be restored if you create a new account using the same Apple ID.
