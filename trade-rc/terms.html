3. Description of Service

Trade RC ("Trade Relay Chat") is an 18+ gay social and dating app built around IRC-style text chat rooms. Its tagline is "Talk first. Match later." The App runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac. One purchase covers all three, and a few features are available only on iPhone and iPad. Features include: public rooms (user-created or system-seeded), private invite-only rooms, and permanent owned rooms; slash commands and channel modes; the Floor (a grid of nearby profiles); daily match suggestions; Taps, Codes, Boosts and Spotlight; Viewed You; presence indicators; end-to-end encrypted direct messages; Room Companions; and widgets. Plus and Premium subscriptions, consumable Codes and Boosts, and one-time Companion purchases are optional. The App is for social and entertainment purposes for adults 18 and older.

3.1 What Other Users Can See About Your Activity

Other users may see that you are "Online now," "Recently active" or "Offline" unless you turn on Appear offline. Plus subscribers can see who viewed their profile ("Viewed You"); you can opt out with Hide me from Viewed You. In direct messages, other people see read receipts ("Read 2m ago") and typing indicators. In rooms, they see typing indicators. There is currently no way to turn off read receipts or typing indicators. See the Privacy Policy, Section 8a.

3.2 Encrypted Direct Messages

Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted by default. Your keys are stored on your devices and, if you use it, in iCloud Keychain. We do not hold your keys and cannot recover encrypted messages. If you sign in on a new device without iCloud Keychain, earlier encrypted messages will become unreadable. If the other person has never published a key, text messages to them are sent unencrypted and marked with an open-lock "Not encrypted" badge. Photos are never sent unencrypted.

3.3 Rooms, Room Owners and Operators

Room Owners and Operators can moderate their rooms: /kick (a 5-minute timeout), /ban (24 hours, 7 days or permanent), /mute (1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days or indefinite), /op and /deop (Room Owner only), and channel modes. The modes available depend on your subscription tier: Free accounts can use +i (invite-only); Plus adds +t (topic lock) and +v (verified users only); Premium adds +s (secret) and +m (moderated). In a +m room, only the Room Owner and Operators can post. In a +v room, only verified users, the Room Owner and Operators can post; our server enforces this, and the App shows a notice to people who cannot post. Room Owners can delete a room, which deletes its message history. Temporary rooms that users create may be frozen and archived after 24 hours if they stay small, which makes them read-only and hides them from the room list; archiving does not delete their messages. Room moderation is separate from TRP's moderation: Room Owners are not our agents, and we are not responsible for their decisions. Room Owners and Operators must follow these Terms. Moderation actions are logged.

3.4 Messaging Limits

To reduce spam, you can send one first message until the other person replies. Photos can be sent only after a conversation is accepted. Free, unverified accounts can start up to 5 new direct-message conversations per day with people they haven't matched with. Taps are limited to 30 per hour. We may change these limits.

8. Reporting, Blocking, and Moderation

The App includes user-controlled safety tools and human moderation by TRP. These tools reduce risk but do not eliminate it, and we do not promise that they will detect or prevent any particular conduct.

  • Block. You can block any user from their profile. Blocking works in both directions: it hides profiles, prevents matching and stops direct messages. In rooms, /ignore hides a user's messages on your device only, and blocked users' room messages are not filtered.
  • Report. You can report a user from a profile, room or DM. Categories: harassment, hate speech, threats, scam/spam, sexual content sent unsolicited, impersonation, underage user, and other (rooms add "off-topic"). Reports include a category and an optional note. They do not automatically include message text or screenshots, so describe the problem in your note. Because DMs are end-to-end encrypted, we cannot read reported DM content unless the message was sent unencrypted. A human reviews reports, and we aim to prioritize threats of violence, child-safety issues and doxxing, but we do not guarantee any response time or outcome.
  • Automated signals. If three or more different people block an account, or three or more report it, within 7 days, the account is flagged for human review, and its ability to start new DMs may be limited. We do not ban accounts automatically. Public-room messages are not scanned automatically; they reach a human only through a user report.
  • On-device checks. Incoming DM text is checked on your device by an abuse classifier that may label a message "May be abusive." Repeated hits from the same sender show a banner with one-tap Block and Report. Outgoing DM text is not screened. When Apple's Sensitive Content Warning is turned on, Apple's Sensitive Content Analysis checks photos you send and receive: adults get a "send anyway?" warning before sending a flagged photo, and sending is blocked under Apple's child-protection mode. Incoming photos may be blurred, and photos in message requests are always blurred. This analysis never leaves your device, and nothing is blocked automatically except as stated above.
  • Room moderation. See Section 3.3.
  • Mercy. Mercy is a silent filter that screens output from the HEDDA-6 Companion. It does not post, and it is not a room moderator.
  • TRP moderation. TRP may remove User Content from public surfaces, warn, suspend or ban accounts, restrict features, withhold or revoke purchases, and refuse service at our sole discretion, without prior notice for serious violations. We have no obligation to monitor User Content and are not liable for moderating or failing to moderate it.
