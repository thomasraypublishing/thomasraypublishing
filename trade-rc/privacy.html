Introduction#

Thomas Ray Publishing LLC ("we," "us," "our") makes Trade RC (the "App"), a social and dating app for adults 18+ built around IRC-style chat rooms. This policy explains what information we handle, why, who receives it and how long we keep it. It covers the App on iPhone, iPad and Mac. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

1. Summary#

  • We collect your sign-in credential, the profile you create, an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km on your device before upload), the messages you post, activity signals such as presence and profile views, safety reports, and purchase records from Apple.
  • Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted by default. We can see who messaged whom and when, but not the content, except for text sent to someone who has no encryption key, which is marked "Not encrypted."
  • Optional sensitive details (HIV status, PrEP use, last-tested date, exact birth details, attachment style, private body-type preferences) are stored in an owner-only record on our servers that only your own account can read, and on your device. They are never shown to other users, never used for matching or filtering, and never sent to our chat provider, to Anthropic or to analytics. The one exception is the optional Cosmic feature, which is set to Fully enabled by default: once you add your birth date, a broad summary worked out from it (Chinese zodiac animal, element and life-path number) is shared with other users' apps, which use it to show a cosmic compatibility reading and as a small part of the compatibility score. Choosing "Sun sign only" stops sharing it, and turning off the Chinese zodiac or Numerology layer leaves that layer's values out. Your birth time and place are never shared. Turning Cosmic off removes the summary. See our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (Section 17).
  • Your profile views are logged so Plus subscribers can see who viewed them (you can opt out). Logs are deleted after 30 days.
  • Omega-1 (Premium) sends only what you type to it, your chat nickname and your own recent Omega-1 conversation to Anthropic. It never sends other members' room messages (Section 9a).
  • Analytics are on by default and can be turned off. They are tied to a random per-install ID, which we also store with your account's device record.
  • We do not sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising, show ads, or include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not use your content to train AI models.

4. How We Use Information#

We use information to: run rooms, the Floor, daily matches, DMs and Companions; authenticate you; show your profile; rank matches and the Floor (Section 9); process purchases; operate safety features (blocking, reporting, human moderation, on-device photo analysis, the on-device abuse warning on DMs you receive, screening of AI Companion output, and the review flag described in Section 8b); communicate with you; fix bugs; enforce our Terms; and comply with law.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, track you across other companies' apps or websites, or use your messages, photos or profile to train AI models.

7. Photos, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, and Location#

All permissions are optional, and you can change them in your device settings (iPhone/iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security; Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security).

  • Photos. The App uses Apple's out-of-process photo picker, so we receive only the photos you choose. All metadata, including EXIF and GPS, is removed by re-encoding before upload. If Apple's Sensitive Content Warning is on, profile photos are checked on your device; a flagged photo can't go on your public profile. Results never leave your device. Photos you add to your private album are stored with your account and are not visible to other users in this version.
  • Camera. Used only for the optional verification selfie (on Mac, through the system camera picker, including Continuity Camera). An on-device face check may warn you but does not block submission. A human reviews the selfie in our staff console. It is stored in a private storage bucket the App cannot read back, is deleted once approved or rejected, and is erased at purge if still pending. We do not create or keep a face template.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Used only to record an optional profile voice prompt, which is not available in this version. No audio is uploaded. The App has no voice notes or voice rooms. The Mac app does not request microphone access.
  • Contacts (iPhone and iPad only). Used only to pick one contact for "Share Location During Dates" (Settings → Safety & Security). We do not upload your address book.
  • Location (When In Use only; no background location). The App rounds your location to about 1 km on your device before upload, as described in Section 3, and our servers use it only to calculate distance. We never receive your precise GPS coordinates. "Share Location During Dates" (iPhone and iPad only) takes one precise reading, creates an Apple Maps link and pre-fills a message to one contact you choose. You must tap Send, and the message travels through Messages. No location, contact or message text is sent to or stored by Trade RC, and it is not live tracking. The App does record an analytics event that you used the feature (the duration you chose, whether you added a note, and the outcome), which follows the Share Analytics setting described in Section 12.

8. Messages, Rooms, and User Content#

Rooms. Room messages are visible to room members and stored by Stream with no automatic expiry. They are removed when the room owner deletes the room; rooms with no members for 24 hours may also be deleted. User-created temporary rooms that stay small may be frozen and archived after 24 hours, which makes them read-only and hides them from the room list; archiving does not delete their messages. Private invite-only rooms are hidden from the room list but are stored the same way. If you delete your account, your room messages remain, attributed to "Deleted User."

Direct messages. DMs and DM photos are end-to-end encrypted by default using keys created on your device and stored in your Keychain (synced through iCloud Keychain if enabled). When a DM is encrypted, we cannot read its content. We and Stream see metadata: sender, recipient, time and size. You can compare safety numbers, and a banner appears if a contact's key changes; sending is refused, not downgraded, if a known key disappears or changes. Exception: if the other person has never published a key, text DMs to them are sent unencrypted and marked "Not encrypted," and our chat provider can read them. Photos are never sent unencrypted. Push notifications never contain DM text. If you set up a new device without iCloud Keychain, earlier encrypted messages become unreadable, and we cannot recover them. We keep an overall count of encrypted versus unencrypted DMs, with no user identifiers, to monitor encryption coverage.

On-device safety checks. Incoming DM text is checked on your device by an abuse classifier that may label a message "May be abusive." Two or more hits from the same sender show a banner offering Block and Report. Outgoing DM text is not screened. When Apple's Sensitive Content Warning is on, photos you send and receive are analyzed on your device: adults are asked "send anyway?", sending is blocked under Apple's child-protection mode, and flagged incoming photos are blurred. When it's off, photos from people you haven't matched with are blurred. Photos in message requests are always blurred. None of this analysis leaves your device.

When we may read room (not DM) content: when someone reports a room or a message in it; to respond to valid legal process; and to investigate abuse or fix technical problems. Public rooms are not scanned automatically. We do not read messages for advertising.

8a. Activity Signals Other Users See

  • Viewed You. When you view a profile, we log the view on our server. Plus subscribers can see who viewed their profile. View logs are deleted automatically after 30 days. Turn on Hide me from Viewed You (iPhone/iPad: Settings → Privacy; Mac: Settings → Privacy & Safety) to stop your views from being shown.
  • Presence. Others see "Online now," "Recently active" or "Offline." Appear offline (same Settings location) hides this. Recent activity also affects the order of matches and the Floor.
  • Read receipts and typing indicators. DMs show "Read" times and typing indicators; rooms show typing indicators. You can't currently turn these off.
  • Taps, Codes, Boosts and Spotlight. A Tap shows the recipient who sent it and may trigger a push notification. A Code (with optional note) notifies the recipient and places you at the top of their deck for up to 14 days. A Boost places your profile at the top of its distance band on the Floor for 60 minutes. A Spotlight is a free swipe that counts as a Like and places you near the top of the other person's next daily deck; you can use one per rolling 24 hours.
  • Other controls. "Participate in Matches" and "Who can message you" limit who sees and contacts you.

8b. Safety Signals, Reports, and Moderation

  • What a report sends: reporter, reported user, where it happened (profile, room or DM), category, optional note, and room or conversation ID. No message text or screenshots are sent automatically. A DM report also flags the specific message at Stream, but it stays encrypted, so moderators can't read it unless the message was sent unencrypted.
  • Review flag: if three or more different people block an account, or three or more report it, within 7 days, it is flagged for human review, and its ability to start new DMs may be limited. We do not ban accounts automatically.
  • Room moderation: room owners and operators can kick, ban and mute; these actions are logged on our server.
  • Blocking: works in both directions and is private. Room /ignore is local to your device.

8c. Law Enforcement and Legal Requests

We may disclose information we hold in response to valid legal process, to protect someone from imminent harm, or to enforce our Terms. We report apparent child sexual exploitation to NCMEC as federal law requires. We cannot disclose the content of encrypted DMs because we cannot read it.

9. AI Features and Personalization#

These features run on your device using Apple's Foundation Models, and nothing from them leaves your device: icebreakers, "Why this match," "Polish my bio," nick suggestions, channel "Summarize" recaps, HEDDA-6 replies, and the personalization summary. Omega-1 is the one exception (Section 9a).

Companions. HEDDA-6 reads the most recent messages in the current room on your device and generates a reply, which is screened before it posts. Mercy is not a character and never posts. It is a silent filter that screens HEDDA-6's output for slurs, scams and toxicity on your device, backed by a server check on Companion posts for off-platform contact and payment requests. Host, Dot, Sage, Prompt, Keep and T-Bot (our staff bot in #welcome) use pre-written lines. Those lines were written in advance with Anthropic models through an offline process that used no user data, and you never trigger a live AI call when they respond.

How matches are ranked. Our server scores daily matches using distance, looking-for overlap, how recently someone was active, profile completeness, verification, Codes and Spotlights received, and a 20% random selection. It also leaves out people you have already liked or passed. Floor order uses distance band or presence, then active Boosts, then profile completeness. If you and another person both use Cosmic, each device also compares the other person's broad cosmic summary with its own. That comparison appears in the match details and adds a small share to the compatibility score worked out on your device, which can change the order of your matches, and which are kept, when "Matches tailored to you" is on. The server's ranking does not use it. The server does not use your swipe history to learn your preferences; that happens only on your device, as described below.

"Matches tailored to you" (on by default). Your device keeps a private local record of swipe outcomes (kept 90 days) and of rooms you share with others (kept 180 days). It uses them to reorder your deck and to create a short on-device summary of your swipe preferences using Apple Foundation Models. Nothing leaves your device. Turn this off in Settings → Privacy (iPhone/iPad) or Settings → Privacy & Safety (Mac); capture stops immediately.

We never use your content to train any AI model.

9a. Omega-1 — Third-Party AI (Premium Only)

Omega-1 is available to Premium subscribers in public rooms with /omega. The first time, a consent screen names Anthropic, and Omega-1 is unavailable until you accept (on Mac too).

What is sent to Anthropic when you summon Omega-1:

  • the instructions that define Omega-1;
  • your chat nickname;
  • your new message;
  • your own recent exchange with Omega-1 (up to 4 exchanges, or 8 messages, within the last 20 minutes).

Other members' room messages are not sent. No user IDs are sent. Anything you type or paste into your prompt, including other people's words or names, is sent to Anthropic as written.

Retired features. Omega-1 no longer offers room recaps or saved room notes. Room notes saved earlier have been deleted from our live systems.

Storage. Your /omega prompt and Omega-1's reply are posted publicly in the room and are stored by our chat provider (Stream) like any room message. Our database keeps only a per-user usage counter for the limits (150 messages per day, 3,000 per month), and our logs keep operational details such as status codes, approximate token counts and room identifiers, but not the text of prompts or replies. Anthropic processes requests under its own terms and privacy policy.

Omega-1 is a fictional AI character, not a person or a professional. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (U.S.) or contact local emergency services.

10. Purchases#

Apple processes all payments; we never receive card details. Apple sends us transaction records, and each purchase includes your Trade RC account identifier (appAccountToken), so purchases belong to your Trade RC account, not just your Apple ID. Codes and Boosts balances are stored on our server with your account and appear on all your signed-in devices. Companion ownership is stored with your account and never shown to other users. A new account created after deletion cannot restore an old account's Companion purchases.

11. Notifications and Widgets#

There are ten categories, each with its own toggle: daily matches, direct messages, mentions, mutual match, Code received, Tap received, verification approved, subscription, account-deletion reminders, and Companion requests. On iPhone and iPad, a Notification Service Extension adds sender details for Communication Notifications and inline reply. It keeps usage counters that follow the Share Analytics setting. DM pushes never include message text. iPhone shows a Live Activity for the match drop. iPhone and iPad offer a Home Screen widget (match count, unread DMs). Mac offers a desktop widget and a menu bar extra. Widget and menu bar data is stored locally on your device. Quiet Hours (Do Not Disturb on Mac) and per-room silence schedules mute in-app banners only, not push notifications. You can turn off all notifications in your device's notification settings.

12. Analytics and Diagnostics#

On by default. We collect app and OS version, device model, locale, time zone, accessibility settings, network type, App Store country or region, screen views, feature counts, error summaries, and MetricKit crash and performance data (iPhone, iPad and Mac). Records are keyed to a random per-install ID and stored in our CloudKit public database. We also store that ID with your account's device record, which we use to send you notifications, so these records can be connected to your account while it exists. We use them to understand how the App is used and to fix problems, never for advertising. When your account is permanently deleted, the device record is deleted and nothing on our servers connects the analytics records to you, except feedback reports you sent, which include this ID and any email you gave. Turning off Share Analytics (Settings → Privacy on iPhone/iPad; Settings → Privacy & Safety on Mac) stops collection everywhere, including MetricKit forwarding, the hang monitor, the notification extension and widgets. When you turn it off, the App first tries, for up to two seconds, to send one last record that notes you turned it off, along with any records already waiting to be sent. After that, nothing more is collected.

Deleting analytics: copy your analytics ID in the App (Settings → Privacy → Your Analytics ID on iPhone/iPad; Settings → Privacy & Safety on Mac) and send it through the feedback form (or email contact@thomasraypublishing.com, subject "Analytics deletion request"). We will delete matching records, usually within 30 days. Do this before uninstalling, because the ID is lost when the App is removed. There is currently no in-app "clear my analytics" control.

13. Feedback and Support#

Feedback (iPhone/iPad: Settings → About & Help → Send feedback; Mac: Settings → About → Send Feedback) is stored in our CloudKit container. It may include your optional email, an optional diagnostics attachment (app and OS version, device model, accessibility settings, recent error counts, battery and thermal state, your App Store country or region, and your subscription and purchase status: tier, active product identifiers, renewal or expiry date, whether a grace period, billing retry or Family Sharing applies, how long you have subscribed in broad ranges, your Codes balances, pending-purchase counts, and whether our server's subscription record matches the App's) and optional screenshots you attach. Screenshots may contain whatever is on your screen, so review them before sending. Every report also includes a few counts about your device's message-encryption state, with no keys or message content.

14. Children#

Trade RC is for adults 18+ only. Age is self-confirmed (year of birth plus a confirmation), and our server rejects birth years under 18. We do not independently verify age. If we learn a user is under 18, we will remove the account. Report suspected minors in the App or at contact@thomasraypublishing.com.

15. Retention, Deletion, and Restore#

Delete your account: on iPhone or iPad, Settings → Account → Delete account; on Mac, Settings → Account → Delete account….

Immediately: your profile is hidden, your chat identity is renamed "Deleted User," chat access is revoked, you are signed out everywhere, and we ask Apple to revoke Trade RC's Sign in with Apple link (we retry with a server job).

Days 0–30: signing back in restores everything, including purchases. If you used Apple sign-in, you'll sign in with Apple again.

After day 30 (permanent): your login is permanently disabled; profile photos, private-album photos and any pending verification selfie are erased; your chat identity is scrubbed; and your account and its database records, including your optional private details, are deleted from our live systems. Any passkey credential we stored is deleted. Direct messages you authored in one-to-one conversations, and their attachments, are deleted from our chat provider by a server job after the purge. Room messages you posted stay, anonymized as "Deleted User." Messages other people sent you are not affected.

Kept after deletion: room messages you posted (anonymized as "Deleted User"); reports about you, with identifiers removed; account-lifecycle audit events; records we must keep by law (for example, child-safety reports); and Apple's own transaction records. Analytics records are kept until you ask us to delete them; after your account is permanently deleted, nothing on our servers connects them to it except feedback reports you sent (see Section 12). Backup copies of deleted information roll off on our providers' normal backup schedules.

Account deletion does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in your Apple account (iPhone/iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions; Mac: App Store → Account → Subscriptions).

16. Your Choices and Rights#

You can edit most information in your profile, change settings, revoke permissions and delete your account at any time. You may also ask us to access, correct, delete or provide a copy of your information. Exports are available by email request only; there is no in-app download. Our staff do not view your optional private details while operating the App; the only built-in way for staff to retrieve them is an audited account-data export. If your account is suspended, email contact@thomasraypublishing.com and we will process the deletion. To make a rights request, email contact@thomasraypublishing.com with the subject "Privacy Rights Request." We will verify your request using your account email or by signing in, and we aim to respond within 45 days. Where state law gives you a right to appeal a decision, reply to our response, or email us with the subject "Privacy Appeal." Requests about your optional health details follow the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (Section 17).
