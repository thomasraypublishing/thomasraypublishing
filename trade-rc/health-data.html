Trade RC — Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 6, 2026 · Effective Date: October 17, 2026

Introduction

Thomas Ray Publishing LLC ("we," "us," "our") makes Trade RC (the "App"), a social and dating app for adults 18+. This policy explains how we handle your consumer health data. It supplements our Privacy Policy and is provided under the Washington My Health My Data Act (Chapter 19.373 of the Revised Code of Washington), Nevada's consumer health data law (Chapter 603A of the Nevada Revised Statutes), and similar laws.

1. Consumer Health Data We Collect, and Why

CategoryWhat it includesWhy
Optional sexual-health details HIV status, PrEP use and the date you were last tested, only if you choose to enter them Keep them for you, show them back to you in your own profile settings, and restore your profile if you reinstall the App or switch devices
Health information you write yourself Anything about your health that you type into a bio, a message, a Code note or an Omega-1 prompt Deliver the content you chose to send to the people you chose to send it to

We do not use these details for matching, ranking, filtering, advertising or analytics. We treat the other optional private details described in our Privacy Policy, such as attachment style, in the same way, except the broad Cosmic summary described in Section 9 of our Privacy Policy, which is shared once you add your birth date while Cosmic is fully enabled (its default).

2. Where It Comes From

Only from you. You provide your optional sexual-health details by entering them in the Sexual Health section of Edit Profile. We do not collect consumer health data from other companies, from your device's health records, or by inferring it from what you do in the App.

3. Who Can See It

  • You. Your optional sexual-health details are stored in an owner-only record on our servers that only your own account can read, and on your device.
  • Other users cannot see it. These details are never shown on your public profile or to anyone else in the App, and they are not used for matching or filtering.
  • Our chat provider, Anthropic and analytics never receive it. These details are not sent to Stream (our chat provider), to Anthropic (which generates Omega-1 replies) or to our analytics.
  • Our staff do not view these details while operating the App; the only built-in way for staff to retrieve them is an audited account-data export.
  • Health information you write yourself goes to whoever can see or receive that content. A bio is public, a room message is visible to the room, a direct message goes to the person you send it to, and an Omega-1 prompt is sent to Anthropic and posted publicly in the room.

4. Sharing and Sale

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it with any third party. Our database provider, Supabase, Inc. (USA), stores it for us and processes it only on our behalf, as our service provider.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we collect, use or share your consumer health data.
  • Access your consumer health data, along with a list of all third parties we have shared it with. There are none.
  • Withdraw consent. If you have given us consent to collect or share your consumer health data, you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Delete your consumer health data. We will delete it from our live systems and tell our service provider to do the same. Backup copies roll off on our providers' normal backup schedules. If your account is suspended, email contact@thomasraypublishing.com and we will process the deletion.

You can change or clear your optional sexual-health details yourself at any time in the Sexual Health section of Edit Profile. You can also delete your account (iPhone/iPad: Settings → Account → Delete account; Mac: Settings → Account → Delete account…), which permanently removes them after the 30-day restore window.

6. How to Exercise Your Rights

Email contact@thomasraypublishing.com with the subject "Consumer Health Data Request." Tell us which right you want to use. Please do not put health details in the subject line or send your password. We will verify your request using your account email or by signing in, and we will respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we may extend that period once by up to 45 days and will tell you why.

7. Appeals

If we decline your request, we will explain why. You may appeal by replying to our response or by emailing contact@thomasraypublishing.com with the subject "Consumer Health Data Appeal." We will respond to your appeal within 45 days and explain our decision in writing. If your appeal is denied, you may contact your state Attorney General:

  • Washington: Office of the Attorney General, https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint
  • Nevada: Office of the Attorney General, https://ag.nv.gov/Complaints/File_Complaint/

8. Changes to This Policy

We will update the Last Updated date and notify you in the App or by email before material changes take effect. Where the law requires your consent to a new collection or sharing of consumer health data, we will ask for it before that collection or sharing begins.
