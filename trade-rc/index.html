*** MOTD — the rules of the server

Private by architecture.

/mode +privacy

  • Coarse location only — rounded to a coarse grid on your device before anything is sent
  • Sign in with Apple as the primary login; your Apple ID stays private
  • Analytics are anonymous, and there's an off switch in Settings
  • Profile photos are screened on-device with SafetyKit before they reach our servers

/mode +safety

  • Block and report on every profile and message surface; mute any room or conversation
  • 18+ self-attestation required at sign-up
  • Every report lands in a moderation queue for human review

/mode +a11y

  • Full VoiceOver support
  • Dynamic Type at every size, including AX1–AX5
  • Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast, Bold Text
  • Color-independent UI — we test every release against these settings
