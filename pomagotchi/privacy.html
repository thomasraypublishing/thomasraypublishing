Pomagotchi · Privacy

Pomagotchi Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026 · Version: 1.13 · App: Pomagotchi (iOS / iPadOS / watchOS) · Publisher: Thomas Ray Publishing LLC

0. Privacy at a Glance

  • We don’t operate any servers. TRP has no Pomagotchi user account system, no login, no friend list backend.
  • We don’t collect personal information about you. No name, email, phone, address, IP address, advertising ID, device ID, or location ever leaves your device on our account, unless you choose to fill in our optional Feedback form.
  • We don’t sell or share your personal information. We never have, and we have no business model that would. No targeted advertising, no cross-context behavioural tracking, no data brokers, no monetisation of user data of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs. No analytics SDKs, no ad networks, no attribution, no tracking. Pomagotchi does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • AI runs on your device. Mood text, sentiment, vision, translation, and personality features use Apple’s on-device frameworks (Apple Foundation Models, Vision, Natural Language, CoreML, Translation). They do not call cloud AI services. Pomagotchi does not send prompts or generated content to any server — not Apple’s Private Cloud Compute, and not ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or any other third-party AI service.
  • Your cloud saves are yours. Game progress and Pom Stasis backups live in your iCloud (Apple’s service governed by Apple’s privacy policy). We cannot read them.
  • Two small cloud surfaces we do operate, both using Apple CloudKit:
    • Anonymous analytics (opt-out at any time): minimised, non-identifying counters about how the app is performing. No name, email, IP, location, pet name, or other identifier.
    • Feedback form (only when you tap “Send Feedback”): contains whatever you choose to write, and is stored in TRP’s own Apple CloudKit container.
  • Children: the app’s DATA handling is designed to be safe for users of any age — we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. (This is a statement about data, not about content: the App is rated 12+.) Section 16 explains additional protections.

This Privacy Policy explains how Thomas Ray Publishing LLC (“TRP”, “we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the Pomagotchi application for iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS (the “App”), distributed worldwide through the Apple App Store.

It applies to the App and to the supporting pages on thomasraypublishing.com. It does not apply to Apple’s own services (iCloud, Game Center, the App Store, WeatherKit, MapKit, HealthKit, Apple Foundation Models, Apple Intelligence). Apple’s handling of any information collected through those services is governed by Apple’s privacy policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

The App is rated 12+ on the App Store. It is designed to be a mixed-audience product and is not part of the Apple Kids Category.

3. The Architecture in One Paragraph

TRP operates no servers. The App stores everything on your device by default. Optional cloud surfaces use Apple’s iCloud (which is your own iCloud account, not ours) and Apple’s CloudKit (where TRP has one small, narrowly-scoped container of its own, iCloud.PomPet.Analytics, which holds anonymous analytics and, only if you submit the Feedback form, your feedback). All AI, machine-learning, vision, and audio analysis runs on your device.

4. What We Collect — By Surface

4.1 On the device (no transmission to TRP)

The vast majority of what the App processes never leaves your device:

  • Your pet’s name, appearance, level, mood, inventory, in-game gems, mini-game scores, trophies, accessory state, and personality state.
  • Your environment, motion, sound, and visual inputs while features that need them are active (see Section 6 — Permissions).
  • AI-generated text (Paper Pom Letters, mood text), sentiment analysis output, sound classification labels, vision results, personality engine outputs.

Nothing in this list is transmitted to TRP.

4.2 In your iCloud (TRP cannot read)

With your Apple ID’s permission, the App writes to two areas of your iCloud:

  • iCloud Key-Value Storage (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore) — gameplay progress and preferences, so your Pom syncs between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
  • GameSave cloud saves (Apple’s GameSave framework on iOS 26+, stored in your iCloud Documents) — “Pom Stasis” snapshots of your pet’s full state.

These data live in your Apple iCloud account. They are governed by Apple’s privacy policy and are protected by your Apple ID security. TRP has no ability to read them. If you sign out of iCloud, those features stop syncing.

4.3 In TRP’s CloudKit public container — minimised analytics

If you have anonymous analytics enabled (it is on by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings ▸ Share Anonymous Analytics), the App writes one minimised record at most once every 24 hours to a public CloudKit database that TRP controls. Each record contains:

  • A per-install random deduplication ID (generated locally; not linked to your Apple ID, your name, your email, your device’s advertising ID, or any other identifier; rotated if you reinstall or reset).
  • A one-way SHA-256 digest of an opaque iCloud-account-scoped identifier (CKContainer.userRecordID). This digest lets TRP count a single human as one person across that human’s iPhone, iPad, and reinstalls of Pomagotchi, instead of double-counting each device or each reinstall. It is not your Apple ID, name, email, IDFV, IDFA, DeviceCheck identifier, or any directly identifying value. The underlying token is minted by Apple, is scoped to TRP’s Pomagotchi CloudKit container, and cannot be reversed to your Apple ID. We hash it client-side before transmission and store / transmit only the digest. The digest is recorded only when you are signed in to iCloud at the moment of analytics submission; otherwise this field is omitted and only the per-install ID is recorded.
  • App version, iOS/iPadOS/watchOS version, device model class (e.g., “iPhone15,3”), screen size, locale, time zone offset.
  • Aggregate counts: session count, days active, action counts, mini-game scores, gem totals, trophy count.
  • Health signals: crash-free session rate, memory-warning counts, error summaries (counts and category codes, no message bodies, no stack traces with user content).
  • Feature toggles, subscription status flag and tenure measured in days only, Watch-paired flag, widget-active flag.
  • Accessibility settings state (added Build 357): which iOS accessibility settings are switched on (VoiceOver, Switch Control, Reduce Motion, Bold Text, Increase Contrast, Differentiate Without Color, Reduce Transparency, Invert Colors) and your chosen text size — so we can tell whether accessibility improvements are reaching the players they were built for. This is declared as Other Diagnostic Data in the App Store’s app privacy label and in the app’s privacy manifest.
  • Mini-game results measured against VoiceOver (added Build 363; disclosed here from Version 1.11): summary results for the mini-games — the game played, how rounds ended (completed, lost, timed out, or abandoned), round scores, round durations, and win counts — tallied separately for rounds played while VoiceOver was running on that device. We collect it for one reason, and it is the reason the accessibility work exists: a mini-game that sighted players finish and VoiceOver players abandon is a mini-game we have not actually made playable, and knowing only that VoiceOver was switched on cannot show us that. These are running totals and summaries, never a diary of your sessions: there is no record of an individual round, no timestamps, no inputs, and nothing describing you. It is tied only to the anonymous analytics identifier described in this section, is covered by the same Share Anonymous Analytics opt-out, and is declared as Product Interaction and Other Diagnostic Data (Analytics purpose, not linked to you) in the App Store’s app privacy label and in the app’s privacy manifest.
  • Purchase activity, by App Store storefront (added Build 346): aggregate counts of purchase-funnel events (purchase started, purchase completed, purchase cancelled, welcome-offer redeemed) and which storefront saw a player’s first purchase, all keyed by the App Store storefront country (Apple’s Transaction.storefront — your App Store account’s billing region, not your device’s location). We use this solely to check whether regional pricing changes are reaching players in the markets they target. There is no price, no currency, no amount, no transaction ID, no receipt, and no payment-method information in this record. This is declared as its own Purchase History data type in the App Store’s app privacy label and in the app’s privacy manifest (see Section 12).
  • Additional non-identifying counters for forward-compatible product analytics.

This record does not contain your name, email, phone, address, IP address, precise or coarse location, pet name, written letter content, photo, audio, video, contacts, health data, payment-method or billing details, or any other identifier that could reasonably be used to identify you. We design the analytics record to minimise the possibility of identifying you, but we recognise that pseudonymised data combined with technical fingerprints could in principle single out an individual user. We therefore treat these records as personal data where applicable law requires us to do so, including in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and any other jurisdiction whose law treats pseudonymised data as personal data. We use these records solely to understand whether the App is working and which features are used so we can fix bugs and prioritise improvements. We do not sell, share, or use them for advertising, profiling, or cross-context behavioural advertising of any kind.

Analytics records are retained for up to 13 months and then automatically purged (see Section 19). You can disable anonymous analytics in Settings ▸ Share Anonymous Analytics. When disabled, no record is written.

4.4 In TRP’s CloudKit container — Feedback form only

Only when you tap Send Feedback and submit the form, the App writes a single record to TRP’s own Apple CloudKit container (iCloud.PomPet.Analytics), the same container that holds anonymous analytics. It is not shown to other players, and we use it only to triage bugs and reply to you. The fields are exactly what you type:

  • Your feedback message (free text).
  • Optionally, your name.
  • Optionally, your email (so we can reply).
  • Optionally, a diagnostic report generated locally (app version, iOS version, recent error codes, and recent app-activity breadcrumbs including in-app purchase events — which pack, and the App Store storefront region, never prices, receipts, or payment details — never your contacts, location, or pet content). The diagnostic report also includes the same per-install random ID and one-way account digest described in Section 4.3, so that we can match your report to the crash and error diagnostics needed to fix your issue.
  • Optionally, up to three screenshots that you pick using Apple’s PhotosPicker.

Because a feedback submission can contain your name or email together with the per-install ID and account digest, those identifiers are linked to your contact details inside that one feedback record — and only there. Analytics records (Section 4.3) never contain your name or email, and remain not linked to your identity. If you leave the name and email fields blank, your feedback is not linked to your identity at all. The form states this plainly beneath the contact fields before you submit.

If you are signed out of iCloud, the App falls back to opening Apple’s Mail composer with the same payload pre-filled, and nothing is uploaded to CloudKit.

We use feedback solely to triage bugs and respond to you. We do not use it for advertising or share it with anyone. You may request deletion of a feedback submission at any time by emailing contact@thomasraypublishing.com. See Section 11 for the special handling that applies to feedback from users under 13.

5. What We Explicitly Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your real name, email, phone number, mailing address, or any account identifier (unless you voluntarily type them into the Feedback form).
  • Your IP address (we run no servers that would see it; Apple may see it for CloudKit/iCloud, governed by Apple’s privacy policy).
  • Your precise or coarse geographic location.
  • Your contacts.
  • Your photos or videos (we only see screenshots you pick and attach to a feedback submission).
  • Audio recordings or transcripts (Pomagotchi has no microphone access; every sound in the game is synthesized on your device).
  • Camera frames (Teleport AR and gesture features run entirely on-device).
  • Health or fitness data (step count is read on Watch on-device only; never transmitted).
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not request App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • Device identifiers (we use no identifierForVendor, no identifierForAdvertising, no DeviceCheck identifier for tracking, no fingerprint).
  • Payment credentials, billing details, or transaction receipts. All purchases go through Apple StoreKit; TRP never receives your card number, billing address, or a transaction/receipt identifier. (If analytics is enabled, we do record aggregate purchase-funnel counts and the App Store storefront region of a purchase — see Section 4.3 — but never a price, an amount, or any payment-method or receipt data.)
  • Biometric identifiers, government identifiers, neural data, immigration status, sexual orientation, religious beliefs, political opinions, racial or ethnic data, trade-union membership, or any other special-category / sensitive data.
  • Children’s personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from any user, including children under 13 (see Section 16).

6. Permissions and What Each Is Used For

The App requests the following iOS permissions only when a feature that needs them is first used. You can revoke each in iOS Settings at any time. The exact prompts shown to you are:

  • Location When In Use — “Pomagotchi uses your location to bring real weather to your Pom’s world, detect places like parks and Disney parks for special encounters, and let Nearby Friends know when you’re around. Your location is processed on your device. It is never sent to TRP.” Used with Apple’s WeatherKit and MapKit. Coordinates do not leave your device on our account; Apple sees them under its own privacy policy.
  • Bluetooth (optional, OFF by default) — “Pomagotchi uses Bluetooth so two phones in the same room can let their Poms meet, peer-to-peer. There is no server and no internet involved.” See Section 7.
  • Motion & Fitness — “Pomagotchi uses motion to detect shakes and steps, so your Pom can play and earn walk bonuses. Motion data is processed on your device and is not stored after the session.”
  • Camera — “Pomagotchi uses the camera for Teleport AR (placing your Pom in your world) and to recognise hand gestures. Camera frames are processed on your device in real time and are never recorded or saved.”
  • Add to Photos — “Pomagotchi can save Photo Booth pictures of your Pom to your Photos library when you tap Save.” Add-only; we cannot read your library.
  • HealthKit (Watch) — “Pomagotchi reads your step count on Apple Watch so your Pom earns bonuses for walks. Step count stays on your device and is never sent to TRP or included in analytics.”
  • Notifications — “Pomagotchi sends local notifications scheduled on your device. We do not operate push servers.”
  • Siri & Search — “Pomagotchi lets you ask Siri to feed, play with, groom, check on, and otherwise care for your pom hands-free, and surfaces those quick actions in Spotlight search.” Every App Intent runs on your device using Apple’s on-device speech and intent frameworks. The words you say to Siri and any text you type into Spotlight are processed by Apple under its own privacy policy and never reach TRP. The donation of App Intents to Spotlight and Siri uses non-sensitive item names (pet name, accessory name, trophy name) only — see Section 14.

Picking screenshots for Feedback uses Apple’s PhotosPicker, which does not require a Photo Library read permission and does not give the App access to anything except the screenshots you pick.

7. Nearby Friends (Bluetooth peer-to-peer)

Nearby Friends is off by default. When you turn it on, your iPhone uses Bluetooth Low Energy to advertise and discover other phones running Pomagotchi nearby. The exchange happens directly between phones; there is no server.

The payload exchanged between two phones is limited to: your Pom’s display name, variant, level, current mood tag, and a rotating ephemeral peer ID. We do not exchange your Apple ID, real name, contacts, location, IP address, or any other identifier. Friendship and “pack” records are stored locally on your device and, if iCloud sync is enabled, in your own iCloud Key-Value Storage.

You can tap Reset Pom’s Social Identity in Settings to rotate the ephemeral peer ID and clear all friendship and pack records.

8. AI and On-Device Processing

Pomagotchi uses Apple’s on-device ML frameworks:

  • Apple Foundation Models (the on-device ~3-billion-parameter language model included in iOS 26) for Paper Pom Letters, mood narration, and other text generation.
  • Apple Natural Language for sentiment and tagging.
  • Apple Vision for AR placement, gesture recognition, and photo aesthetics.
  • CoreML for the personality engine.
  • Apple Translation (on-device machine translation) for translating your Pom’s speech and letters into your device language. The text being translated never leaves your device; if a language pack is not yet installed, you choose whether to download it, and that download comes from Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

All of these run on your device. Pomagotchi does not send prompts or generated content to any server — not Apple’s Private Cloud Compute, and not ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or any other third-party AI service. The Apple Foundation Models that power Pomagotchi’s text features run entirely on-device and never offload your prompts to Apple’s servers; Private Cloud Compute is Apple’s own first-party infrastructure for Apple Intelligence and is not available to third-party apps such as Pomagotchi. Pomagotchi also does not train any AI model on your data. Inputs and outputs of these models stay on your device unless you choose to share them (for example, sharing a Paper Pom Letter via iOS Share Sheet, in which case the receiving service’s policy governs).

When your device writes your pom’s diary or status lines with Apple’s on-device model, it may mention moments your pom remembers, such as your first walk together. This happens on your device.

9. iCloud Sync (your iCloud, not ours)

When you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud Drive enabled, the App uses two Apple-provided mechanisms to keep your pet across your devices: NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore for small preferences and progress, and Apple’s GameSave framework for full save snapshots (“Pom Stasis”). These data live in your iCloud and are governed by Apple’s privacy policy. TRP has no access to them. To disable, sign out of iCloud or turn off iCloud for Pomagotchi in iOS Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud.

See Section 4.3 above. Analytics can be turned off in Settings ▸ Share Anonymous Analytics. We rely on this signal to fix crashes and improve gameplay. We will never use it to identify you, and we have no technical means of doing so. Records are retained for up to 13 months (see Section 19).

If we become aware that feedback was submitted by a child under 13 and contains additional personal information, we will delete the entire submission and notify a parent if a parent contact is available.

12. Subscriptions, In-App Purchases, and Promo / Offer Codes

The “Fluff Society” auto-renewable subscription and the consumable coin packs are sold by Apple through StoreKit 2. Apple processes your payment. TRP never sees your name, email, billing address, payment-method information, or transaction identifiers. The App verifies a receipt on-device only and stores a subscription-status flag locally. Anonymous analytics records the subscription status and tenure in days, and counts of purchase-funnel events keyed by the App Store storefront country (your App Store account’s billing region, reported by Apple’s StoreKit Transaction.storefront, not your device’s location) — but not amounts, prices, currencies, dates of birth, or receipts. This purchase data is aggregate, tied only to the anonymous analytics identifier described in Section 4.3, and used solely to check whether regional pricing is reaching players in its target markets. It is never used for tracking. Gems are earned through gameplay only; they cannot be purchased with real money.

Promotional / offer codes (currently App Store Connect promo codes, transitioning to Apple Offer Codes on 26 March 2026) are redeemed through Apple. The redemption count appears in anonymous analytics as an integer; the code itself never reaches TRP.

For payment, billing, refunds, and family-sharing questions about subscriptions, please use Apple’s standard channels (Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions, or reportaproblem.apple.com).

13. Apple Watch

The Pomagotchi watchOS app reads your step count via HealthKit on the Watch only, read-only, and only to award in-game walk bonuses. Step count is not transmitted to TRP and is not included in anonymous analytics.

14. Spotlight Indexing

The App donates non-sensitive items (such as your pet’s name as it appears in the app, your accessories, and trophy names) to iOS Spotlight so you can search them from the home screen. This index is stored on your device by Apple and is not transmitted to TRP. You can disable Spotlight indexing for the App in iOS Settings ▸ Siri & Search ▸ Pomagotchi.

15. Notifications

The App schedules local notifications on your device. We do not operate push servers and do not use Apple Push Notification service for marketing.

20. Security

We rely on Apple’s platform security (Secure Enclave, device encryption, Sign in with Apple iCloud security, CloudKit transport encryption) for all data in transit and at rest. TRP-side access to our CloudKit container is through the developer account, which is protected by Apple ID with two-factor authentication. We do not maintain any other systems that hold user information.

Breach notification. In the unlikely event of a personal data breach affecting data held in our CloudKit container, we will notify affected users without undue delay and report the breach to the relevant supervisory authority where required. For users in the EU/EEA and the UK, we commit to notifying the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of a notifiable breach, as required by GDPR Article 33 and UK GDPR Article 33. For users in jurisdictions with other timelines (e.g., Quebec Law 25, Brazil LGPD, U.S. state breach laws), we will comply with the applicable statutory timeline.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting personal information, we will notify affected users and relevant supervisory authorities as required by applicable law.
