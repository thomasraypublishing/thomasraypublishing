16. Bluetooth Nearby Friends (Forward-Looking Disclosure)

Pomagotchi includes engineering for a forthcoming Nearby Friends experience using Bluetooth Low Energy peer-to-peer encounters. This experience is currently feature-flagged off and may not be active in your build; this Section provides advance notice for when it ships.

  • Optional, off by default. You will be asked to opt in before any Bluetooth activity begins.
  • No servers. Encounters happen directly between nearby devices; TRP operates no matchmaking server.
  • Payload exchanged: pet display name, variant, level, current mood tag, and a rotating ephemeral peer identifier.
  • Never exchanged: your Apple ID, real name, contacts, location, IP address, or any personally identifying information.
  • Rate limits: 6-hour per-peer cooldown, 8-encounter daily cap. A Pom Pack auto-forms at three Pack-tier peers.
  • Reset. “Reset Pom’s Social Identity” in Settings rotates your ephemeral peer ID and clears all friendship/pack records.
