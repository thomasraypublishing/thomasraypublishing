Description of Service

Pomagotchi is a virtual-pet game in which you adopt, name, and care for one or more cartoon Pomeranian companions across six coat-color variants (Pom Pom Classic Orange, Snowball Cream White, Midnight Black, Cocoa Chocolate Brown, Maple Sable, Ember Red Rust). The App’s currently shipped features include:

  • Daily care actions (feed, groom, play, walk, bathe, brush, bedtime) with a stat/decay system and XP/leveling progression;
  • The Fluffpedia in-app Pomeranian/Spitz encyclopedia;
  • Fluffpuffy Cove, a 13-episode serialized cozy-mystery visual novel; episode access can be unlocked with the in-game Season Pass purchased using Pom Coins;
  • Dog Show competitions with simulated AI judging across four tiers;
  • Mini-games including Puff Pop (match-3, approximately 1,000 levels), Treat Catch, Dig for Treasure, Memory Match, Agility Course, and Hide and Seek;
  • Photo Booth with scene backgrounds that ship with the app (including exclusive Royal Palace, Sakura Temple, and Northern Lights scenes for Fluff Society members);
  • Trading Cards with card backgrounds and flavor text that ship with the app, and an exclusive Royal Dancheong theme for Fluff Society members;
  • Paper Pom stationery letter composition;
  • Augmented Reality experiences using the device camera and on-device Apple Vision;
  • Location-aware environment detection (home/cafe/gym/beach/Disney-park geofencing) for ambient context;
  • Lock-Screen Live Activities, rich notifications, and Siri Shortcuts;
  • Apple Watch companion app offering a SNES-era pixel-art “alternate universe” experience that syncs from the iPhone;
  • Nearby Friends, an optional Bluetooth feature that is off until you turn it on in Settings;
  • Game Center leaderboards and achievements; Spotlight indexing; iCloud Key-Value sync (opt-in, ON by default); GameSave framework cloud preservation (iOS 26+).

A visionOS companion is not part of the App today.

Bluetooth Nearby Friends

Pomagotchi includes Nearby Friends, an optional experience in which your Pom can meet other Poms whose owners are nearby, using Bluetooth Low Energy peer-to-peer encounters. It is off until you turn on “Let Poms Meet Nearby” in Settings → Nearby Friends, and you can turn it off again at any time.

  • Optional, off by default. No Nearby Friends Bluetooth activity begins until you turn it on.
  • No servers. Encounters happen directly between nearby devices; TRP operates no matchmaking server.
  • Payload exchanged between devices: your Pom’s name, coat color, level, current mood tag, and a random peer identifier that is not linked to your Apple Account and changes when you reset your Pom’s social identity. Your Pom’s name is shared as you typed it, so choose a name you are comfortable with nearby players seeing.
  • Other players’ Pom names are checked on your device. A name is checked against a blocklist and, on devices with Apple Intelligence, by an on-device model; a name that fails, or that cannot be checked, is replaced with a generic name before it can appear in your Pom’s diary.
  • Never exchanged: your Apple ID, real name, contacts, location, IP address, or any personally identifying information.
  • Rate limits: 6-hour per-peer cooldown, 8-encounter daily cap. A Pom Pack auto-forms at three Pack-tier peers.
  • Reset. “Reset Pom’s Social Identity” in Settings generates a new peer identifier and clears your friendship, Pack and encounter records.
