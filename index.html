App III · iOS

Hush Hush
Snap Snap — silent camera.

A silent-capture camera for the moments a shutter sound would ruin. Pets, wildlife, theaters, ceremonies — capture cleanly. Control completely.

  • Three independent cue toggles — visual, haptic, and audio each on or off separately
  • Show Mode and Dark Ride Mode for concerts and dark rooms — free
  • Launch from the Lock Screen — Camera Control, AirPods stem click, Volume Down
  • Rule-of-thirds grid, horizon level, and Steady Snap with live stillness indicator
  • Hushed Pro: smile and palm triggers, Auto Snap, Manual Mode, RAW and ProRAW, night modes, Cinematic video
  • Ultra Snapped: adds the Snappy Kit — on-device tools from Text Grab to Transfer Hush

IVAbout the House

An independent publisher
for small wonders.

We make curious little things — books, art volumes, and apps that feel like they arrived from a nearby, fluffier universe.

Thomas Ray Publishing LLC is an independent publisher bringing creative stories, art, and digital experiences to life. From illustrated children's books to art collections and interactive apps, we craft experiences that spark joy and imagination.

Rooted in a love for Pomeranians, art, and storytelling, our catalog spans the playful adventures of Captain Henry Gingersnaps, curated visual art collections, our virtual pet companion Pomagotchi, the mystical The Device, the silent camera Hush Hush Snap Snap, and a lineup of iMessage sticker packs for everyday delight.
